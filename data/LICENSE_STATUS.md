# Data redistribution status

The initial public repository does not distribute the candidate-level score tables, AF2-derived labels, aggregate benchmark/source tables, RFdiffusion-derived representative structures, manuscript, or publication figures.

These materials remain in the local release candidate with hash-verified provenance, but their copyright, contributor approval, and relevant upstream redistribution terms have not yet been confirmed. They can be added in a later release after the project owner approves a data/figure/manuscript license and confirms the obligations for RFdiffusion-derived structures and upstream target coordinates.

The repository's MIT License applies to project-owned original software only. It does not license these withheld research materials or third-party content.
